FAQ
Before you flash anything
Will it work on my head unit?
If the unit runs Android 9 or newer, yes. Everything is drawn for a landscape screen with large targets. On Android 13 and up some vendors restrict launcher replacement, so behaviour varies by device.
Do I need root or a custom ROM?
No. Sideload the APK and set it as your home app. Only the optional system build needs AOSP platform keys — most Chinese units ship with them.
What does Pro actually add?
Navigation running inside the launcher instead of throwing you into another app, offline maps, custom map themes and speed camera alerts. Everything else stays free.
Does it need Play Services?
No. The free build runs fine without Google services. Pro is distributed through the Play Store, but its maps work offline.
What data does it collect?
None. No ads, no analytics, no accounts. Location never leaves the device and is only read while navigating.
Can I go back to my old launcher?
Any time. Clear the default home app in Android settings and your stock launcher comes straight back.
Put it on your head unit.
Sideload the debug APK, set it as your home app, and reboot. Android 9 (API 28) and newer. No account, no Play Services required.
